Main Dashboard Navigation

Managers need a clear way to navigate between:

1. Probation
2. Personal Development

Probation should only show people currently on probation.

Personal Development should show every member of the manager’s team.

Summary Section at the Top

At the top of the main Manager Dashboard landing page, please show:

Team Summary

Total people in team
This already exists, keep it.

Probation Summary

Show the following cards:

People in probation
Number of people currently on probation. This already exists, keep it.

People pending reviews
Number of people on probation who are within 7 days of a scheduled review date.

The review dates should be set when a new starter is created in the Admin Dashboard.

People with published reviews
This should be counted by individual, not by review date.

Logic:
An individual should count as having a published review if:

* their review date has passed, and
* they have a published review completed.

They should count as pending review if:

* they are within 7 days of a review date, and
* the review has not yet been published.

Please do not count each of the 4 probation review dates as separate people. The dashboard should count individuals.

Personal Development Summary

Show the following cards:

Active development plans
Number of people who:

* have selected their current role, and
* have started scoring their competencies within the last 3 months.

Passive development plans
Number of people who:

* have selected their current role, but
* have not logged in or scored competencies for over 3 months.

Missing development plan
Number of people who have not logged in and selected their current role.

Tooltip Logic

For every summary card, please add a small “i” or “?” icon.

When the manager hovers over it, show the logic behind that number in plain English.

Team Member Table

Under the summary cards, add a table showing every team member.

Columns:

* Name
* Current Role
* Current Role Competencies (%)
* Target Role
* Target Role Readiness (%)
* Financial Target

Logic:

Current Role
Show the role selected by the individual as their current role.

Current Role Competencies (%)
Display the percentage of competencies completed for the individual’s current role assessment.

Target Role
Show the role selected by the individual as their target role.

Target Role Readiness (%)
Display the individual’s current readiness percentage against their target role competency assessment.

Financial Target
Show the financial target entered by the individual. If they have not entered one, or the role does not require one, leave this blank.

Use the same colour logic already used in the Individual Dashboard:

* Green = target achieved
* Orange = near target
* Red = not near target

Please also add a small “i” or “?” icon beside each column heading so managers can hover over it to understand how each value is calculated.
