PROBATION MANAGEMENT SECTION

Create a dedicated Probation Dashboard within the Manager Dashboard.

Purpose:
Allow managers to easily identify, support and track all employees currently in probation.

Display:

• Employee Name
• Role
• Start Date
• Current Probation Month
• Next Check-In Due Date
• Overall Probation Score
• Probation Status:

* On Track
* Needs Support
* At Risk
* Passed
* Failed

Manager Actions:

• Schedule Check-In
• Complete Check-In
• Record Notes
• Assign Actions
• Publish Feedback

Probation Check-In Form

For each probation review managers can score:

Skills
Knowledge
Behaviour
Results

Score each area:

1 = Below Expectations
2 = Partially Meeting Expectations
3 = Meeting Expectations
4 = Exceeding Expectations

Manager records:

• Strengths
• Development Areas
• Support Required
• Agreed Actions
• Review Outcome

The system should automatically maintain a history of all probation reviews and display progress over time.

Managers should be able to quickly see:

• Who is due a probation review
• Who is overdue a probation review
• Who is at risk
• Who is ready to pass probation

PROMOTION OBJECTIVES SECTION

Create a dedicated Promotion Dashboard for employees working towards their next role.

Purpose:
Allow managers to coach, monitor and document progress against promotion objectives.

Display:

• Employee Name
• Current Role
• Target Role
• Promotion Readiness %
• Number of Objectives Complete
• Number of Objectives Outstanding
• Next Promotion Check-In Date

Manager Actions:

• Complete Promotion Check-In
• Update Progress
• Add Evidence
• Assign New Actions
• Publish Feedback

Promotion Check-In Form

For each objective display:

• Objective Description
• Success Criteria
• Target Date
• Employee Progress Update
• Manager Feedback
• Evidence Attached
• Status:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* Completed
* Exceeded

Managers should be able to record:

• Check-In Date
• Notes
• Strengths Demonstrated
• Development Areas
• Actions Agreed
• Overall Promotion Recommendation

Promotion Recommendation Options:

• Not Yet Ready
• Progressing Well
• Nearly Ready
• Ready for Promotion

AUDIT TRAIL

Maintain a full history of all:

• Probation Reviews
• Promotion Check-Ins
• Feedback Published
• Actions Assigned
• Actions Completed

Managers and Admins must be able to view historical records at any time.

MANAGER HOME PAGE

Create three manager action panels at the top of the dashboard:

1. Employees Requiring Probation Reviews
2. Employees Working Towards Promotion
3. Employees Requiring Development Support

Show counts and quick links so managers immediately know who needs attention when they log in.
